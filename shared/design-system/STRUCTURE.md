## Documentation boundaries

- Design System Knowledge: meaning, usage rules, relationships, and guidance
- Figma: visual assets and specifications
- Code: implemented behavior and APIs
- Product Knowledge: product-specific behavior and business rules
- Product Work: temporary proposals and unreleased changes
