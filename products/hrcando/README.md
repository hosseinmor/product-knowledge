# HRCanDo Product Knowledge

```text
hrcando/
├── overview.md
└── areas/
```

`overview.md` explains the suite or product boundary, users, main Areas, and major journeys.

`areas/` contains one practical document for each meaningful HRCanDo Product Area. Keep related flows, rules, permissions, states, edge cases, unknowns, and sources together.

Use Shared Product Concepts or shared product rules only when their meaning and ownership are genuinely shared across modules or products.

Use:

```text
templates/product-overview.md
templates/product-area.md
```
