# Kando

## What this product group is

Kando is the HR product group that contains four distinct products:

- ATS
- Pulse
- Onboarding
- Learning

Each product has its own users, boundaries, Product Areas, and workflows. Shared concepts or services should be documented once under `shared/` only when their meaning is genuinely consistent across products.

## Products

### ATS

Supports hiring operations, including recruitment requests, approval workflows, jobs, and candidate management.

Path:

```text
products/kando/ats/
```

### Pulse

Supports organization-level employee feedback and survey experiences. Its exact product boundary and Product Areas require owner review.

Path:

```text

```

### Onboarding

Supports employee onboarding experiences and administration. Its exact product boundary and Product Areas require owner review.

Path:

```text

```

### Learning

Supports organizational learning and development experiences. Its exact product boundary and Product Areas require owner review.
