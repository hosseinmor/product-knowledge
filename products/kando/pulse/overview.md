# Pulse

## What this product is

Kando Pulse supports organization-level employee feedback and survey experiences. Current product discussions indicate that the organization owns the survey process and receives aggregated results, while participant responses are expected to remain anonymous.

## Primary users

- HR and People teams
- Organization administrators
- Managers who consume authorized aggregate results
- Employees who participate in surveys

Formal roles and result-access permissions require owner review.

## Problems it solves

To be completed by the Product Area owners.

## Product boundaries

### In scope

- Organization-level survey setup and participation
- Anonymous response collection
- Aggregated reporting and organizational insight

### Out of scope

- Personal self-assessment products unless explicitly part of Pulse
- ATS, Onboarding, and Learning behavior

## Main Product Areas

Initial candidates:

- Survey and Campaign Management
- Participant Access
- Response Collection
- Results and Reporting

These candidates require owner review.
