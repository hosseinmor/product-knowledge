# PM Research with Product Knowledge

## Purpose

Use Product Knowledge as internal context for AI-assisted product research without confusing current product facts with external research or recommendations.

## Inputs

- Research question
- Product name
- Relevant Product Area when known
- Optional data, research, competitor, or market sources

## Workflow

1. Read the product `overview.md`.
2. Read the smallest relevant set of Product Area documents.
3. Read related shared Product Concepts or product rules when they affect the question.
4. Read relevant Design System or content guidance only when the research concerns interaction, UI, or communication.
5. Summarize what is already known about the current product.
6. Identify gaps, unknowns, and assumptions.
7. Perform the requested external or internal research.
8. Compare findings with the current product context.
9. Present opportunities, risks, and questions for the PM.

## Output

Keep these sections separate:

```text
Current product context
Known gaps and assumptions
Research findings
Comparison and implications
Recommendations
Questions requiring product decisions
```

Do not present an external benchmark or AI recommendation as current product behavior.
