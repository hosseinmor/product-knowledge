# PM Research with Product Knowledge

## Goal

Use Product Knowledge to provide internal context before external research or benchmarking.

## Process

1. Identify the product and relevant Product Areas.
2. Read the Product Overview and the smallest relevant set of Product Area and Shared Product Concept documents.
3. Separate current internal knowledge from unknowns.
4. Conduct external research only after internal context is clear.
5. Present findings in two sections:
   - What we know about our product
   - What research or benchmarking adds
6. Mark recommendations as recommendations, not current product truth.

## Output

- Research question
- Relevant current product context
- Known constraints and gaps
- External findings
- Opportunities
- Risks and open questions
